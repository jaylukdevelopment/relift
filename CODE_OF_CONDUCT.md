# Code of conduct

Relift deals with attack tooling, so the people around it have to be unusually
clear about consent and unusually calm in disagreement.

## Expected

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they show otherwise.
- Critique the code, the design or the evidence — never the person.
- Respect the scope of an engagement. Do not ask for, or share, a target you are
  not authorised to test.
- Do not post other people's credentials, hostnames, customer names or scan
  output without their permission. Redact before you paste.
- Accept a maintainer's "no" on a feature that weakens the safety model.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging or assisting unauthorised access to a system.
- Spam, sockpuppetry, or opening issues solely to advertise.
- Security issues posted publicly instead of through [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through the repository's
[private advisory form](https://github.com/jaylukdevelopment/relift/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer, [@jaylukdevelopment](https://github.com/jaylukdevelopment),
directly. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts. The
response is proportional: a correction and an apology for a first lapse, removal
for repeated or severe behaviour.
